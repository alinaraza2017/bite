## Full description

Limit: 4000 characters. Paste the block below. Do not paste the editor notes.

```
Bite is a calorie, macro, and gym tracker for the way food is actually served: a roti, a pyali of daal, a karchi of curry, a glass of lassi. You can mark the oil in the tadka as Light, Medium, or Heavy.

Bite gives estimates, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a doctor or a dietitian. The numbers are for your own log. They are not a diet prescribed for you.

Foods you can search include roti, tandoori roti, paratha, naan, puri, and aloo paratha. Rice dishes include plain rice, biryani, beef biryani, pulao, daal chawal, and khichri. Curries include nihari, haleem, karahi chicken, mutton karahi, chicken qorma, aloo gosht, keema, and paya. Daal and sabzi include chana masala, daal mash, daal masoor, bhindi, and aloo palak. Drinks include chai, lassi, doodh, and dahi. Snacks and sweets include samosa, pakora, chapli kebab, seekh kebab, gol gappay, halwa puri, jalebi, and gulab jamun. Eggs include boiled, fried, and omelette.

Search understands everyday names. Typing chapati finds roti. Portions use household units such as roti, pyali, karchi, and glass.

Today shows a calorie ring, protein, carbs, and fat, meals from breakfast to snacks, and net calories after a workout.

The gym log stores sets. The next session can show last time's weight and reps. When you finish, Bite estimates calories burned from the duration and your body weight. That is a simple formula, not a lab test.

Progress shows a 7-day calorie chart, a weight chart, weekly averages, and a streak. You can log weight in small steps. Kilograms and pounds are a display choice. Weights are stored in kilograms.

Onboarding asks sex, age, height, weight, how active you are, and whether you want to lose, maintain, or gain. Targets are estimated on the phone. You can edit the profile later. A lose goal is estimated as 500 kcal below maintenance, and a gain goal as 300 above. If that would fall below the estimated resting burn, the target stays at the resting burn. These are planning numbers only.

There is no Bite account. Meals, workouts, and weight stay on the phone. There are no ads and no analytics. You can export one JSON file and share it yourself from the phone, or reset the logs.

Bite Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription sold by Google Play. The price you see in the app is the price Play shows in your currency. Premium lifts the free limit of 10 custom foods and opens the chart past 7 days.

Nutrition values are estimates. A restaurant portion, a packet, or your own recipe can differ. Bite gives estimates, not medical advice.
```

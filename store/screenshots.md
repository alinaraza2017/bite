# Screenshots

Capture these from your own phone or emulator, using a build that matches the listing. Draw any overlay words yourself. Do not download store screenshots, food photos, or mockups.

> **Verify on the upload box:** how many phone screenshots are required, the file type, and the pixel size. The project spec asks for 4 to 8 screenshots, a 512 × 512 icon, and a 1024 × 500 feature graphic. Those sizes are from the spec. Confirm them in Console before you export.

Take the shots in order so the gallery reads as one story. Crop out the debug banner. The **Premium (demo)** switch only exists in a debug build. Use a release build from the testing track if you can, so that switch is not in the Settings shot.

## 1. Today, with one meal logged

**State:** Onboarding already finished. Log one roti (or two) at lunch so the ring, the three macro bars, and the meal row are visible. Leave some of the day empty so the screen still has space.

**Show:** the calorie ring, eaten and remaining, and the roti row.

**Overlay:** Log roti. See what is left.

## 2. Portion sheet

**State:** Open nihari or karahi chicken. Set the unit to pyali or karchi. Tap an oil chip (Light, Medium, or Heavy) so the live calories change.

**Show:** the unit chips, the oil chips, and the calorie number on the add button.

**Overlay:** Pyali, karchi, and the oil in the tadka.

## 3. Search

**State:** Open Log food. Type `chapati`. The list should show roti (and tandoori roti if it matches). Do not use an empty search.

**Show:** the search word and the roti result.

**Overlay:** Search chapati. Find roti.

## 4. Gym set

**State:** A workout in progress with at least one set filled in. If you have a previous session, the "last time" hint should be visible. Start the rest timer only if it fits without covering the set.

**Show:** exercise name, weight, reps, and the last-time hint if you have one.

**Overlay:** Last weight and reps, ready to repeat.

## 5. Progress

**State:** At least a few days with food logged, plus two weight entries, so the 7-day bars and the weight line are not empty. Stay on 7 days. The 30-day control can show its lock. That is an honest free-tier shot.

**Show:** the 7-day calorie chart and the weight line. Include the streak only if it is not zero.

**Overlay:** Seven days of eating and weight.

## 6. Premium

**State:** Open Premium while the phone is online and the two subscriptions are active in Play Console, so the cards show Play's real prices (rupees or whatever the phone's store country uses). Do not shoot the "Loading prices" line or the error line. Do not shoot a debug build's demo switch and call it the paywall.

**Show:** Bite Premium, the two prices, and Restore purchases.

**Overlay:** Monthly or yearly. Price from Google Play.

If the products are not active yet, skip this shot until they are. A fake price would not match the app.

## Also required, not screenshots

- **App icon, 512 × 512.** Your own drawing. Confirm the size on the upload box.
- **Feature graphic, 1024 × 500.** Your own drawing. A cream background with the word Bite is enough. No downloaded food photography.

The medical line does not need its own screenshot. It must stay in the full description and on the Settings screen: "Bite gives estimates, not medical advice."

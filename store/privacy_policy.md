## What Bite is

Bite is a calorie, macro, and gym tracker. It estimates targets and food energy on the phone. Bite gives estimates, not medical advice.

## What Bite does not do

- Bite has no sign-up and no Bite account.
- Bite has no ads.
- Bite has no analytics.
- Bite does not run a server that receives your meals, body details, or workouts.

## What stays on the phone

Bite stores your data in the app's private storage on the device (a local database and a small settings file). That includes:

- Profile: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and goal. These are used only to estimate calorie and macro targets.
- Food logs, custom foods, workouts, sets, and body-weight entries.
- Whether premium is on, and the time Google Play last answered that question.
- Your choice of kilograms or pounds for labels.

Uninstalling Bite removes that private app storage from the phone. **Reset data** in Settings deletes logs, workouts, weight entries, and custom foods on the phone. Seeded foods and exercises stay.

Bite does not ask for your name, phone number, or email.

## JSON export

Settings includes **Export data**. Bite writes one JSON file and opens the Android share sheet. You pick where it goes (for example a file app or an email app). The file can contain your profile, food logs, custom foods, workouts, sets, weight entries, unit choice, and whether premium is on.

Bite does not upload that file. If you send it to another app, that app's own policy applies, not this one.

## Premium and Google Play

Monthly and yearly Bite Premium are sold by Google Play, not by Bite. Google processes the payment. Bite does not receive your card number, your Google password, or a copy of your bank details.

The app asks Play whether the Google account on the phone owns a Bite subscription, and saves that yes or no on the phone so a paying person is not locked out offline. Google's own privacy policy covers the Google account and the payment. Confirm the current Google policy link before you add it: [paste the current Google privacy policy URL].

## Children

Bite is not made for children. The store listing is aimed at adults. Do not use it to track a child.
